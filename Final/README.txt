Hello, welcome to HelloWorld!

Thank you for taking the time to visit my web application. Let me discuss a few of the features real quick:

When the webpage is first loaded, you will be able to view all posts, but you will not be able to edit, like, or delete any since you 
do not have an account - that means you should click on the "Sign in" button near the top of the screen. After you click the "Sign in" 
button, you will be redirected to the Sign In page to sign into an account, but since you most likely do not have an account yet, you will
need to make one. There is a link on the sign-in page that will take you to a sign-up page. Once you are on the sign-up page, you can create an
account. Once you make an account, you will be redirected to the sing-in page. After you sign-in, you will be redirected to the home page with 
the additional features of being able to create a post, like a post, and edit/delete your own posts. At the top of the screen, there should be a 
textarea that you can fill so you can make a post - once you're done typing, click the post button right beneath it; congradulations, you have made
you're first post. Find your post by looking through the pages, you now have the options of editing and deleting it. When you click the "edit" button,
a textarea will appear in place of your post content that is autofilled with text from your post content, you can make alterations to the post here
- please click save in order to reflect your changes. Low and behold, you made an edit. In the event you are unsatisfied with your post, you can click 
the "delete" button to permanently remove it. Moreover, you can like any post you see (even your own!): clicking the like button on a post increments
the like counter for that post, futhermore, clicking on the same like button will decrement the like counter to what is was before (this is equivalent
to unliking a post). Lastly, when you are all done, you can sign out of your account and everything will be saved. Feel free to log back in at any time as 
your account was added to the database (for security reasons, your password was encrypted, so rest assured that only you will know it).
      
To run the code, please enter the command "node server.js" in the terminal. A link to the browser window will be printed in the terminal - either copy and 
paste it to the browser or Ctrl+Left Click. It will be running on port 4132.
